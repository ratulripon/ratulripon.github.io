About Me

From ocean systems to environmental change

My academic background is in oceanography, a field that helped me understand the connections between marine systems, coastal environments, climate, and human activities.

Through my academic research, I explored changes in land cover and shoreline patterns using remote sensing and GIS. My research experience also involved environmental data collection, data organization, field activities, and coastal monitoring.

Alongside my research background, my work in data analytics has strengthened my interest in data cleaning, statistical analysis, visualization, and communicating analytical findings.

I want to continue developing my knowledge in environmental science, climate-related challenges, coastal sustainability, and geospatial research.

Featured Research

Research that explores environmental change

My academic project examined changes in coastal land cover and shoreline patterns using geospatial tools and field validation.

Academic Research · 2023

Dynamic Transformations in Land Cover and Shoreline: A Decadal Analysis of Nijhum Dwip, Bangladesh Using Remote Sensing and GIS

This research investigated decadal changes in land cover and shoreline patterns in Nijhum Dwip, Bangladesh, using remote sensing and GIS techniques.

The work included spatial analysis of environmental changes, in-situ field validation, and interpretation of coastal and land-use transformations.
